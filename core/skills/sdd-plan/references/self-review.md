# Substantive PLAN review

Review the draft once after authoring. Fix any material gaps before presenting it. This review checks whether the plan can guide the requested implementation; it does not enforce a fixed document grammar or duplicate the implementation closeout.

## Review prompts

- Does the plan deliver the requested outcome and preserve the stated scope and exclusions?
- Are material acceptance expectations represented in one or more steps?
- Are real dependencies explicit, and can independent work proceed independently?
- Does each step describe an outcome, its limits, and a way to determine completion?
- Are validation activities proportionate to the behavior and risk? Reuse sufficient evidence and avoid duplicate checks without distinct value.
- Are relevant repository facts, inferences, proposals, and evidence gaps distinguishable? Are paths cited only when inspected or clearly marked as proposed?
- For brownfield work, does the plan capture current behavior, intended change, and concrete risks where relevant?
- Are unresolved questions limited to decisions that materially affect behavior, acceptance, scope, access, or implementation? Name the affected work.
- Is the PLAN the only persisted implementation progress source, with no duplicate status in step descriptions or auxiliary files?
- Does the plan avoid speculative code detail, file-only step titles, durations, and unrelated improvements?

Correct substantive omissions. A non-applicable prompt does not require an empty section or placeholder. Do not block the PLAN for section naming, numbering style, a fixed criterion count, or a validator's formatting preference.
