## Document preparation mode

Choose sequential or parallel preparation only when the choice changes how the work will be carried out. Sequential is suitable for dependent stories or a small backlog. Parallel preparation can help independent stories when specialists are available and ownership is clear.

Parallel work is limited to requirement and plan preparation. Do not prepare a dependent story before its declared inputs are resolved. Assign each specialist one story and bounded artifact scope. The parent reconciles results and keeps a single O2 progress table. If specialists are unavailable, continue sequentially in the parent.

There is no required persisted mode flag or CONTINUITY entry. On resume, infer the useful next action from current canonical artifacts and the conversation. Do not ask again if the choice is already clear.
